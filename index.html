About

I am a mathematics student with a deep interest in Abstract Algebra, Topology, and Metric Spaces. This site is my personal canvas for documenting theorems, paradoxes, and the beauty of formal proofs. Everything in mathematics ultimately boils down to having a rock-solid grasp of the basics.

You can read more about my academic background and mathematical interests on my About Me page, or view a curated bibliography of the textbooks and references I use on my Sources page.

Mathematical Notes and Problems

I actively maintain a repository of problems, clever proofs, and mathematical concepts that I find truly fascinating.

  • Interesting Math: A growing collection of beautiful theorems and elegant proofs organized by area (such as the Unique Maximal Subgroup and Elements of a Finite Abelian Group).
  • Problems: I enjoy solving undergraduate-level questions in areas like group theory, ring theory, and point-set topology. Returning to these problems provides a welcome mental break and refines my fundamental knowledge.
