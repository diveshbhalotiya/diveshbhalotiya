  • Problems

    I enjoy solving undergraduate-level questions in areas like group theory, ring theory, and point-set topology.

    Part of the reason I do this is simply for the "dopamine hit"—graduate-level mathematics is notoriously hard, and returning to these problems provides a welcome mental break. More importantly, however, I believe in constantly refining my fundamental knowledge rather than just chasing advanced or "fancy" subjects. Ultimately, when you do rigorous mathematics, everything boils down to having a rock-solid grasp of the basics.
