Group Theory

  • Unique Maximal Subgroup
    A neat proof showing that if a finite group has a unique maximal subgroup, its order must be a prime power. Inspired by a problem in I. Martin Isaacs' Algebra: A Graduate Course.

Topology & Metric Spaces

  • Completeness is Not Preserved Under Homeomorphism
    A demonstration of when the discrete topology does not imply the discrete metric, providing a counterexample to show that completeness is a metric property, not a topological one.
