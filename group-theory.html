Problems in Group Theory

The motive of this problem set is to collect problems that are helpful in developing the theory and verifying whether you understand the concepts or not. Initially, I intended to divide these problems into three parts: easy, medium, and hard. However, "hard" is highly subjective, and what I find difficult, someone else might find trivial. Following a suggestion from my friend Tanush Seal, I decided instead to include problems that specifically helped me understand the theory better and from which I learned something valuable. I will continue to add more questions to this list over time.
