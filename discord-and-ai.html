Farewell to jonorangejuicy's Server

5 October 2026

Today I thought about starting a reading group on a specific subject, so I went to a Discord server I used to frequent, only to find that the owner, jonorangejuicy, had archived it.

Here is the original message from jonorangejuicy:

"Hi everyone, it's the server owner here.

This server has had a good run. But now, I feel it's finally time to archive it. That means no one will be allowed to post any more messages in the main channels. But all the existing channel history and content will remain. In particular, the library channels will still be available for viewing. (I personally still use <#826048888640503838> a lot!)

The reason is simple: With server activity finally winding down, it becomes harder to justify the effort put into monitoring and moderating it, compared to doing other things in life. But no moderation also means not cleaning up the spam that every Discord server gets. So in the end, locking down all the channels is the most efficient choice long-term.

The Human role also loses its purpose, and it is no longer needed to view the channels.

I don't want to get too personal or sappy with this server's origins and history. This server has existed since 2021, and it has been in my life for a significant period. I had many experiences and lessons from here. A lot of them, broadly speaking, showed how hard it is to be a good leader. These are lessons that I will always take with me.

I can only express my gratitude at the many people who have joined this journey with me, putting in their time and enthusiasm - the staff, library contributors, and general members. I hope that many of you have had good experiences too, and I regret not being able to offer better.

Thank you all, and I wish you good luck in your future endeavours!"

It is genuinely sad. I used this server many times for reading groups. Over the past few days, I've observed a similar drop in activity across other Discord servers I frequently use.

Maybe one of the reasons for this decline is AI.

I don’t know in which direction we are all heading, but personally, I don’t like this experience. I suggest that if you have enough time to spend on a problem, do not use AI. Otherwise, it makes no sense.

Mathematics is not always about solving problems; it is about asking new questions, questioning existing concepts, and making ideas finer and finer.
